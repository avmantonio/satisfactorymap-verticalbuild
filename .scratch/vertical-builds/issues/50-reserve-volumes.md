# Reserve volumes
Level: theme
Kind: ticket
Parent: 76
Status: resolved


## Question

How does the scene show an unknown or incomplete object so the uncertainty is visible?

## Answer

A valid `clearance` is a neutral reserve volume, labeled as an approximation, not as the true shell. With no dimensions, show the location and the uncertainty, and do not invent a size. The object is not dropped and is not drawn as an exact solid. Decided 2026-11-23.

## Open follow-up

[61 — Free-form and missing 3D shapes](61-free-form-geometry.md) owns the deferred shape investigation, initial simplified representations and tracked model refinement. The settled approximation rule does not complete that work.

## Approved clarification — 2026-11-26

Unknown scale takes precedence even over a known clearance reserve: show location and scale uncertainty instead of a volumetric approximation. Initial provisional coverage and later refinement follow 48/61.
