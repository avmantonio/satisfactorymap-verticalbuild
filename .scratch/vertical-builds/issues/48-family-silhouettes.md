## Question

What cheap representation lets a person tell production, logistics, power, and support apart, and read their layout?

[Proxy catalog](28-proxy-modeling.md) and [Asset supply](40-asset-supply.md) already constrain which forms are honest and shippable. A reserve or unknown stays out of this kit — [Reserve volumes](50-reserve-volumes.md). Paths are [Static networks](49-static-networks.md).

## Answer

Buildings and structures are simplified silhouettes a person can recognize. Recognizable does not mean the game mesh. Decided 2026-11-23; do not re-grill.
