## Question

What elevation sources are plausible, how faithful are they, what rights and extract size do they carry, and what would it cost to share the factory coordinate frame?

Judge high-resolution aerial imagery explicitly, without assuming it is a reusable mesh. The current `map_highres.png` is Coffee Stain art and stays out of git. Output: a feasibility note and the alternatives.

## Answer

Terrain is a separate investigation and blocks nothing on the factory viewer. The viewer is useful without ground: missing ground hides geographic context, not the relative Z of buildings and connections. Integration is not a ticket unless this study shows a practicable path and it is funded, and that integration would have to switch off. Decided 2026-11-23; do not re-grill.
